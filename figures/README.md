# Figures

Regenerate with `OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 code/make_figures.py` (about 2 minutes, mostly figures 4 and 5). Each figure is written as PNG (200 dpi) and SVG. Captions are drafts. The file numbers are not the figure numbers in the paper, which numbers figures by first appearance: `fig3_madelung_groups` is Figure 1, `fig2_s4_picture` is Figure 2, `fig1_spindle` is Figure 3, `fig4_spectrum` is Figure 4 and `fig5_green` is Figure 5.

1. `fig1_spindle`: Meridian profile of the four-dimensional metric $g^{(4)}_\mu$, circumferential radius $2\mu\sin\theta$ against arc length $\theta$ ($R=1$), for $\mu=1/4,1/2,1$; the dashed lines are the tangent cones at $P_0$, and only $\mu=1/2$ closes up smoothly (slope 1, a round $S^4$), the others have a cone point at both poles. This is a profile plot, not an isometric embedding.
2. `fig2_s4_picture`: The round $S^4$ drawn as a two-sphere: the nucleus $P_0$ ($r=0$) and $P_\infty$ ($r=\infty$) are the poles, the inversion sphere $r=R$ is the equator, and the Kelvin inversion $r\mapsto R^2/r$ is the reflection in the equator that swaps the poles.
3. `fig3_madelung_groups`: (a) The subshells grouped by $N=n+l$, each box as wide as its $2(2l+1)$ states, with the group sizes $\lfloor (N+1)/2\rfloor^2=1,1,4,4,9,9,16,16$ harmonics and $2,2,8,8,18,18,32,32$ states with spin; (b) the period lengths $2,8,8,18,18,32,32$ of the periodic table. The groups are not the periods: for example the second period fills $2s$ (group $N=2$) and $2p$ (group $N=3$), so a period is a block of consecutive subshells, not one group.
4. `fig4_spectrum`: The couplings $Z_NR$ against $N$ for $\mu=1/3,1/2,2/3$: the curves are Eq. (25) of Demkov and Ostrovsky, the points are the numerical shooting values for $l=0,1$ and $n_r=0,1,2$; at $\mu=1/2$ the couplings are $Z_NR=N(N+1)/2$ at integer $N$.
5. `fig5_green`: The closed-form Green's function of Theorem D against the independent partial-wave sum along a ray ($ZR=1.7$, $|x|=R$, angle $70^\circ$, $G<0$ so $|G|$ is shown), with the relative error below on a log scale (errors at rounding level are drawn at $10^{-17}$); the shaded band $|y|\approx|x|$ is omitted because the partial-wave sum converges too slowly there.
